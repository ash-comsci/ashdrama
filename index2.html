THE FALL PLAYNOVEMBER 2026

Sweet fairy tale princesses
go ROGUE.

A hilarious dark comedy that mashes up the royal betrayal of King Lear and Game of Thrones with your favourite fairy tale princesses. When an aging king decides to divide his empire, Cinderella, Belle, and the Snow Queen launch into a ruthless, backstabbing power struggle for the crown. Packed with manipulation, unexpected alliances, and a hilariously high body count, this fast-paced satire is a wildly entertaining ride from start to finish.
